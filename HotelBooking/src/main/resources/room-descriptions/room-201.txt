# The Grand Astoria Deluxe Suite

Step into an enchanting world of cinematic grandeur, nostalgic glamour, and carefully orchestrated extravagance. Designed for two, this spacious Deluxe Suite transforms the romance of pre-war European hotels into an intimate private residence, where sumptuous textures, whimsical Oriental details, and unmistakable Grand Budapest charm meet in perfect symmetry.

Every element has been deliberately composed to feel collected rather than merely decorated: antique-inspired furnishings, dusty pastel tones, polished brass, velvet upholstery, ornate mirrors, and warm, theatrical lighting create an atmosphere that feels suspended somewhere between a 1930s European palace and a Wes Anderson dream.

### The Art of Shared Comfort

The heart of the suite is an imposing **king-size French-style bed**, framed by an extravagant upholstered headboard in rich dusty-pink velvet and dressed in crisp, premium white linens. Layered designer cushions, a sumptuous faux-fur throw, and bespoke bedside details create an indulgent sleeping sanctuary designed for two.

Twin spherical reading lamps sit symmetrically on either side of the bed, while elegant carved nightstands provide discreet space for personal belongings.

The generous bedroom has been designed to feel serene yet theatrical, with pale hardwood floors, a plush cloud-white faux-hide rug, and carefully positioned furnishings that preserve an unmistakable sense of balance and symmetry.

### A Private Salon for Two

Beyond the sleeping quarters lies the suite's **dedicated private salon**, separated from the bedroom to create a genuine sense of privacy and distinction.

A pair of plush velvet sofas and two matching vintage armchairs surround an elegant tea table, creating a refined setting for morning coffee, afternoon tea, conversation, or an evening drink.

Large windows framed by delicate sheer curtains fill the salon with soft natural light, while ornate wall details, antique-inspired artwork, and a statement crystal chandelier transform the room into a miniature private drawing room.

A second **55" Smart TV** is discreetly integrated into the salon, allowing guests to enjoy a film or series without disturbing the peaceful atmosphere of the bedroom.

### The Suite's Signature Details

At the centre of the principal accent wall, an extraordinary **hand-painted golden fan** provides the suite with its most distinctive decorative flourish. Complementary brass detailing, vintage-inspired lamps, framed artwork, and carefully selected Oriental accents continue throughout the rooms.

Two magnificent crystal chandeliers provide the principal illumination, casting a warm golden glow across the high and subtly dramatic ceilings.

The overall composition embraces the deliberate symmetry, playful proportions, romantic colour palette, and nostalgic theatricality characteristic of the hotel's distinctive aesthetic.

### A Dedicated Dressing & Preparation Area

The suite includes a generous **dedicated wardrobe and dressing area**, providing ample space for clothing, luggage, shoes, and personal belongings.

A beautifully crafted vanity table with an upholstered chair sits beneath a large ornate vintage mirror, accompanied by warm theatrical lighting designed to make getting ready feel like a ritual rather than a necessity.

Additional storage and luggage space keep the bedroom and salon uncluttered, allowing the decorative character of the suite to remain the focus.

### In-Room Amenities & Luxuries

**Climate & Comfort:** Central heating and individually controlled air conditioning ensure a comfortable temperature throughout the suite year-round.

**Sleeping:** Premium king-size bed with luxury white linens, designer cushions, plush faux-fur throw, and bespoke bedside reading lamps.

**Living & Personal Workspace:** Private salon with velvet seating, vintage-inspired armchairs, refined tea table, dedicated vanity, upholstered dressing chair, and ornate full-size mirror.

**Entertainment:** Two **55" Smart TVs**, one in the bedroom and one in the private salon, with complimentary premium streaming services including Netflix, Disney+, and HBO Max.

**Refreshment Corner:** A dedicated hospitality station featuring a selection of fine artisan teas, Nespresso coffee machine with daily replenished capsules, elegant tea service, and complimentary still and sparkling Grand Hotel Budapest bottled water.

The refreshment station is located within the private salon, allowing guests to enjoy coffee and tea without disturbing the sleeping quarters.

**Guest Convenience:** High-speed Wi-Fi, USB charging points, international power sockets, telephone, electronic safe, iron and ironing board, and discreet luggage storage.

### The Bathroom Sanctuary

The suite's spacious en-suite bathroom continues the hotel's signature combination of theatrical glamour and practical luxury.

A large **rainfall shower** provides a restorative bathing experience, complemented by a separate handheld shower, premium fixtures, heated towel rails, plush luxury bathrobes, matching slippers, and a professional hair dryer.

Guests are provided with an indulgent selection of **organic botanical toiletries**, including premium shampoo, conditioner, shower gel, body lotion, hand wash, and beautifully presented bathing essentials.

An oversized vintage-inspired mirror and warm ambient lighting complete the room, creating a bathroom that feels as considered as the suite itself.

### A Suite Made for Two

More than simply a larger room, the Grand Romance Deluxe Suite is designed as a **private world within the hotel**. Its separate sleeping and salon areas allow two guests to share the experience while still enjoying their own space: breakfast in the salon, an afternoon spent reading beneath the window, getting ready together at the dressing table, or retreating to the bedroom for an uninterrupted evening.

Every corner is intended to feel theatrical, intimate, nostalgic, and slightly surreal — a place where luxury is not restrained, but **curated with personality**.
