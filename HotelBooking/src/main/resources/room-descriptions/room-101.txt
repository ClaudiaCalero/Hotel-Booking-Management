# The Grand Romance Single Room

Step into an intimate sanctuary of cinematic romance and maximalist sophistication. Meticulously curated for the solo connoisseur of timeless elegance, this mansard single room evokes the palatial charm of pre-war Europe, beautifully balanced with subtle, unforgettable Oriental accents.

### The Art of Personal Comfort

The crown jewel of this private haven is a majestic French-style single bed, featuring a headboard and footboard upholstered in rich, tufted dusty-pink velvet. Dressed in crisp, premium white linens, designer accent pillows, and a sumptuous faux-fur throw, it promises an absolute sanctuary of deep, restorative rest for the solitary traveler.

### Curated Accents

A stunning, hand-painted golden fan watches over your dreams from the main accent wall, while two magnificent crystal chandeliers cast a warm, magical glow across the sloping ceilings.

### A Space for Solitary Indulgence

Enjoy your morning espresso or a late-night read on the refined velvet chaise longue and matching vintage armchair nestled by the large window, wrapped in sheer drapes that softly filter the natural sunlight. The pale hardwood flooring, paired with a plush, cloud-white faux-hide rug, completes a truly sensory luxury experience.

The sleeping quarters are thoughtfully separated from the rest of the suite by a private hallway, creating an elegant sense of distinction and privacy. This corridor leads into a charming adjoining dining and salon area, furnished with comfortable sofas, a refined tea table, and a second television, providing an additional space to relax, read, enjoy refreshments, or unwind away from the bedroom.

A dedicated wardrobe provides generous space for clothing and personal belongings, keeping the sleeping quarters beautifully uncluttered and serene.

### In-Room Amenities & Luxuries

**Climate & Comfort:** Central heating and individually controlled air conditioning to ensure your ideal temperature year-round.

**Living & Personal Workspace:** A finely crafted vanity table with an upholstered matching chair, paired with a large ornate vintage mirror perfect for your morning preparations.

**Entertainment:** A 55" Smart TV seamlessly integrated into the room, equipped with complimentary premium streaming services (Netflix, Disney+, and HBO Max).

**Refreshment Corner:** A curated hospitality tray featuring a selection of fine artisan teas, a Nespresso coffee machine with daily replenished capsules, and complimentary still and sparkling Grand Hotel Budapest bottled water. The full tea and refreshment setup is conveniently located in the adjoining salon area.

### The Bathroom Sanctuary

An en-suite bathroom featuring a rain shower, plush luxury bathrobes, matching slippers, a hair dryer, and a premium selection of organic, botanical toiletries.
